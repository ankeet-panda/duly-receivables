# Duly: direction record

## Brief
- **Owner's words (2026-11-06):** "build a product … both a self-playing product demo and a working product. Show me how modern and high-quality a product you can build for today's market."
- **Answers:** product "New product, you pick" (not caregiving); depth "One screen"; demo "Product page + demo".
- **Mode:** Design (data stays in this browser; no server, accounts, money movement or outside services). Marking an invoice paid records a fact; Duly never moves money.
- **Pick, from research (2026-11-06):** freelancers chasing late invoices. 85% of freelancers are paid late at least some of the time (Remote, State of Freelance Work 2025, via MediaPost June 2026); 29% of invoices paid at least a day late across 100,000+ freelancers (Bonsai invoice data); late payment is the most common complaint on small-business forums (BigIdeasDB 2026, own scrape, not a survey).
- **Job:** When a client's invoice slips past its due date, I want to see who owes me, how unusual the delay is for that client and a reminder in the right tone, so I can chase without the awkwardness and know when money will really land.
- **Who:** Aanya, an independent product designer in Bengaluru, 6–10 live clients, invoices in rupees; laptop at her desk on Monday mornings, phone between calls.
- **Outcome:** in under 10 seconds she knows what is late, which one to chase first and in what tone; the reminder is copied or opened in email in two clicks.

## Five lines
| Line | Answer |
|---|---|
| Product | Duly: a receivables desk for freelancers: what's owed, what's late, when it will land, and the reminder to send |
| Character | Composed, firm-but-kind, quietly confident about money |
| World | The freelancer's desk: a pad of invoice slips with tear-off stubs, a rubber "Paid" stamp, ink on paper. Daylight (light); the desk lamp at night (dark) |
| Moments | 1. The runway: unpaid invoices placed on the day each will most likely land, using each client's own paying habit; 2. the invoice slip and its reminder |
| Signature | The Paid stamp: marking an invoice paid presses a green ink stamp onto its slip, dated, and the slip's amount leaves the runway |

Idea sentence: on Duly, **the rubber Paid stamp on the desk** becomes **the moment money is confirmed**, and **the client's habit** becomes **the forecast**.
